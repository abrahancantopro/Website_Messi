The story of the boy who couldn't grow up and became the greatest.

Messi's history with Argentina

Lionel Messi was born on June 24, 1987, in Rosario, Argentina. From a very young age, he showed great talent for soccer and dreamed of becoming a professional player. As a child, he faced a growth-related issue, and his family decided to take him to Spain so he could continue his treatment and develop as a player. Although he moved to Barcelona,he never forgot his home country and always wanted to represent Argentina. In 2005, at just 18 years old, he was called up to the Argentine national team. His debut did not go as expected, as he was sent off just seconds after entering the match against Hungary. However, that moment did not prevent the start of a long journey with the national team.

Over the years, Messi became one of Argentina's key figures. He played in the 2006 and 2010 World Cups, but Argentina failed to win the title. In 2014, a major opportunity arose: Argentina reached the World Cup final in Brazil, and Messi came very close to winning the trophy, but Germany won 1–0 in extra time. Even more difficult times followed. Argentina lost the 2015 and 2016 Copa América finals to Chile. After losing the second final, Messi announced he was retiring from the national team due to deep disappointment. However, he decided to return and keep fighting for his country. In 2018, Argentina was eliminated from the World Cup in Russia, and in 2019, the team once again fell short in the Copa América. Despite all these defeats, Messi never stopped trying.

A new era began in 2021. Argentina, led by Lionel Scaloni, succeeded in building a team united around Messi. That year, Argentina reached the Copa América final against Brazil at the Maracanã Stadium. With a goal from Ángel Di María, Argentina won 1–0, and Messi secured a major title with the senior national team for the first time. The following year, the team won the Finalissima against Italy and arrived at the 2022 World Cup in Qatar with the dream of winning the only major trophy that had eluded him. The start was difficult, as Argentina lost to Saudi Arabia, but the team bounced back and made it to the final.

On December 18, 2022, Argentina faced France in one of the most thrilling finals in World Cup history. Messi scored two goals, and the match ended 3–3 after extra time. Argentina won on penalties, and Messi was finally able to lift the World Cup trophy. After so many years of defeats, criticism, and lost finals, he achieved the dream he had pursued since childhood. In 2024, he also won the Copa América again with Argentina. Thus concluded a long story of effort, sacrifice, and perseverance: the boy from Rosario who once had to travel to Spain ultimately reached the pinnacle wearing his country's jersey and lifting the World Cup as Argentina's captain.
